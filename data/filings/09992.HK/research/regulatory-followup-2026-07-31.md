# POP MART 09992.HK regulatory follow-up

**Research scope:** regulatory category 1 only

**Evidence cutoff (AS_OF):** 2026-07-29

**Research date:** 2026-07-31

**Method:** ordinary HTTP and local copies of official HKEX filings; no Chrome/CDP

**Issuer:** POP MART INTERNATIONAL GROUP LIMITED / 泡泡瑪特國際集團有限公司

**Listing date:** 2020-12-11

## 1. Strict conclusion

1. **Hong Kong securities/regulatory result:** the completed HKEX issuer-title
   enumeration and the issuer/name searches of SFC, AFRC, HKPF, ICAC and HKLII did
   not identify an issuer-linked sanction, disciplinary action, criminal
   investigation, financial fraud, false financial statement, insider dealing,
   pledge of listed shares, late annual filing or financial restatement through the
   AS_OF date. This is a **qualified negative finding**, not proof of absence, because
   several regulators expose only name/full-text search rather than a stable issuer
   identifier and complete historical register. **Evidence level: medium.**
2. **PRC operating-company penalties:** four administrative penalties involving
   Beijing Pop Mart or its wholly owned Tianjin operating company were identified in
   December 2021 through March 2022: false product-composition advertising
   (RMB200,000), false pricing (RMB50,000), failure to revise a production-safety
   emergency plan (RMB20,000), and failure to disclose prize probability/quantity
   (RMB50,000). These are consumer/operational compliance events, not evidence of
   securities fraud or financial-statement fraud. Original government decision pages
   were not recovered for three events; the fourth is reproduced as an image of the
   government record by credible media. **Evidence level: medium overall.**
3. **Judicial event:** Golden Eagle International brought a derivative-style civil
   claim in August 2020 against Beijing Pop Mart, seeking cessation of stores in
   designated areas and approximately RMB117.2 million. The first hearing occurred
   on 2021-06-17 and the FY2021 annual report said the case remained in process.
   Later annual reports ceased itemising it and only made a materiality-qualified
   statement that there was no litigation with a material adverse effect. The final
   disposition was not located and must not be inferred. **Evidence level: high for
   filing/history; unresolved for final outcome.**
4. **Other allegation:** a January 2026 China Labor Watch investigation alleged
   labour-law and safety failures at supplier Shunjia Toys. POP MART said it was
   investigating and would require remediation if substantiated. This is an NGO
   supply-chain allegation, not a regulator finding against the issuer. **Evidence
   level: medium for the existence of the allegation; unproven as a legal violation.**
5. **Auditor and reporting:** PwC is continuously identified as auditor/reporting
   accountant from the 2020 prospectus through every FY2020-FY2025 annual report.
   The complete 345-title HKEX issuer index contains annual reappointment proposals
   but no change/resignation of auditor title. No annual report was filed late and no
   financial restatement was identified. “Restated” title hits concern articles of
   association, not accounts. **Evidence level: high for the HKEX filing record;
   medium for the broader absence conclusion.**

## 6. Explicit exclusions and interpretation

1. The 2019 `Share Pledge Agreement` is a registered VIE equity pledge by the
   relevant shareholders over their equity in Paqu Huyu in favour of Beijing Pop
   Mart. See FY2025 annual report pp. 62-64. It is **not** evidence that Wang Ning,
   GWF Holding or another controller pledged listed `09992.HK` shares.
2. FY2025 says the group had no pledged assets at 2025-12-31. Restricted cash for
   letters of credit/rental guarantees is not a controller share pledge.
3. The HKLII `[2026] HKCFI 1421` mention of POP MART IPO shares is unrelated to
   POP MART as party or investigated subject.
4. Routine “amended and restated memorandum and articles” filings are not
   financial restatements.
5. Annual-report statements of no litigation with a *material adverse effect* are
   management materiality statements, not an assertion that no proceeding of any
   size exists.
6. AFRC/SFC material mentioning PwC cannot be attributed to POP MART unless the
   engagement or issuer is expressly connected.
